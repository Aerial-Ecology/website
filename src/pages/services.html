Thermal · Bats

Thermal bat emergence and re-entry surveys

Integrated aerial and ground-based thermal surveys for emergence and re-entry, tailored to the structure, its surroundings and the survey objectives.

Drone-mounted thermal cameras give alternative viewpoints and coverage of complex or inaccessible elevations. Ground-based thermal cameras complement them with continuous coverage of selected areas. We can run multiple thermal systems at once on larger or more complex structures. Thermal imaging supports experienced surveyors and professional survey requirements; it doesn't replace them.

Best suited to

  • Large, tall or historic buildings
  • Multiple roof levels and hidden elevations
  • Sites with dense vegetation or restricted access

You receive

  • Reviewable thermal recordings
  • Supporting video evidence
  • Ecological interpretation for your report

Thermal · Birds

Thermal skylark nest detection surveys

Commercial thermal drone surveys that identify and assess potential skylark nest locations in extensive grassland and arable habitats.

Stage 1, night-time detection, identifies and georeferences candidate locations. Stage 2, an early-morning review, assesses thermal and visual observations together and informs any follow-up. Experienced ecological surveyors interpret the results, potential nest locations are kept distinct from confirmed nests, and targeted ground verification can be undertaken where appropriate.

Best suited to

  • Extensive grassland and arable habitats
  • Sites where ground searches are slow or could disturb nesting habitat
  • Projects where ground-nesting birds need consideration

You receive

  • Spatially referenced candidate and confirmed locations, reported separately
  • Supporting thermal and visual imagery
  • A map to guide targeted ground verification

Mapping · BNG support

Drone habitat mapping & BNG support

Accurate, high-resolution spatial data that supports your field habitat surveys and Biodiversity Net Gain work.

Drone imagery and LiDAR make it practical to capture large, complex or hard-to-access sites in detail, and give a dated visual record of the baseline. We integrate this with field survey findings: habitat classification, condition assessment and BNG calculations remain based on surveys in the field by qualified ecologists, with aerial data supporting mapping accuracy and coverage.

Best suited to

  • Large or fragmented sites
  • Sites with limited safe access on foot
  • Projects needing an accurate visual baseline

You receive

  • Orthomosaic imagery
  • Habitat boundaries mapped with field survey findings
  • GIS layers and measured areas in formats that fit your workflow

GIS · Modelling

GIS analysis & ecological mapping

Spatial analysis, suitability modelling and survey visualisation for consultancies.

We turn survey results into clear maps and GIS layers: where the habitat is, how species are likely to use it, and how that relates to the scheme. Modelled outputs support, and are checked against, field evidence.
